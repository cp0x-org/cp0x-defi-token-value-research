# Changes and notes after freezing METHOD_W0.md

W1 (after results, corrected after review). The frozen rule declared T2 and T3 evaluable at n >= 15 but set no limit on degrees of freedom. With 16 observations and 9 category keys, six of them singletons that the fixed effects fit exactly, model V rests effectively on 10 observations in three categories and has 4 residual degrees of freedom. By the frozen rule T2 (b_take = 0.83, one-sided p = 0.0002) and T3 (b_resid = -1.79, one-sided p = 0.006) are inconsistent with Research 5, and ordinary small-sample t-tests with 4 degrees of freedom still reject (T2 two-sided p = 0.009, T3 one-sided p = 0.032). Dropping any one of the 10 effective observations keeps b_take between 0.65 and 0.95. The honest reading is that W0 points against the Research 5 valuation findings in the 2022-2023 regime, but on so few effective observations that it carries little weight next to the main periods (120 observations, b_take = -0.03). The version without category fixed effects (b_take = 0.24, b_resid = 0.02) is a different model and is reported only as a diagnostic.

W3 (disclosure, after review). The feasibility check (check_2022.csv) computed returns against ETH by group before this method was frozen, and the October 2022 window was chosen after seeing them, so the descriptive figures (share beating ETH, median return) were known in advance. The regression variables of T1-T3 were not computed before the freeze. The survivorship check is a lower bound on missing tokens: its universe covers only protocols in today's DefiLlama fee dashboard and protocols DefiLlama marks as dead, so it misses, for example, Serum (about $285M at the start, -3.36 log against ETH) and Multichain (about $76M, -0.72), dead protocol families whose token sits only on the parent, protocols deleted from DefiLlama, and 16 dead tokens whose CoinGecko download failed. The April 2022 count of uncovered tokens is 39, not 37.

W4 (robustness of T1, after review). T1 passes the frozen rule (one-sided p = 0.058) but is fragile: one-sided p = 0.066 with a t distribution and 0.11 with HC3 errors, and dropping LooksRare, Biswap or Osmosis alone takes it above 0.10. It is reported as weakly consistent with Research 4.

W2 (implementation). Research 4 raw downloads for new groups were added to the Research 4 replication working copy (raw/cg, raw/fees), and fetch_fees_children.py was rerun there; it aggregated one new parent (beethoven-x). Existing files were not changed. The frozen Research 4 archive is unaffected.
