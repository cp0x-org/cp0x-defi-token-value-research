I did no on-chain analysis and used no DefiLlama data.

## 2. Scope notes

- **Entity covered.** The 2024 and 2025 figures cover the "Foundations": Lido Labs BORG Foundation, Lido Ecosystem BORG Foundation and Lido Alliance BORG Foundation, plus the DAO-funded committees (LEGO, LOL, CLI, delegate programme) and TRP. They are prepared by Lido Labs Foundation and describe the DAO's grant-funded spending, not a single legal entity's books. The 2023 figures cover only the Lido Contributors Group (RCC / Pool Maintenance Labs Ltd (BVI) / Argo Technology Consulting (Panama)) as reported by Steakhouse.
- **Accounting basis.** The 2025 report says it is "not prepared in accordance with any major GAAP" and is on an accrual basis. TRP is recognised by vesting at the 30-day average LDO price. Period cut-off adjustments were applied only to 2025. The 2023 tables are cash or grant-disbursement actuals, so the methods differ and comparing 2023 with 2024-25 is unsafe.
- **Restatement.** The 2024 figure ($40.9M) is the restated comparative from the 2025 report. I found no standalone 2024 annual report published in early 2025. Steakhouse's Dune "lido-safu" dashboard was the only real-time source then. The dashboard is described in reports but not used here.
- **Gaps.**
  - In 2023, TRP, LEGO, LOL / liquidity incentives, Rewards Share, gas and other committee costs are missing, and so is April 2023.
  - The "Other income and expenses" line (2024 0.9M, 2025 0.1M) is netted inside Total Operating Expenses and is not broken out.
  - Audit and bug bounty costs are inside Operating Expenses.
- **Double counting risks.**
  - LEGO and TRP are separate lines, so do not add them on top of "Operating Expenses" a second time.
  - The 2025 approved-grant list overlaps across Q1 11.1M (Multi-EGG) and the Q2-Q4 Foundation grants.
  - Deposit referrals were moved from Growth & liquidity into Cost of Revenue.
  - "Total Foundations' Expenses" (45.5M / 52.1M) includes Liquidity Rewards, so it is not the benchmark figure.
  - Funds moved to Foundation multisigs but not spent are not expenses. The report says unspent balances on multisigs are counted as treasury.
  - The December 2025 Dune spike was internal transfers (sUSDS conversion, bug bounty reserve multisig), not opex (Olga_K, https://research.lido.fi/t/request-for-detailed-annual-expense-2025/11053).
- **Payment path.**
  - Most spending is not paid directly from the Aragon Agent (0x3e40D73EB977Dc6a537aF587D48316feE66E9C8c). Grants are drawn from the Agent via EasyTrack motions into grantee and committee multisigs, which then pay contributors and vendors. Examples are PML 4/7 multisig 0x17F6b2C738a63a8D3A113a228cfd0b373244633D (9067), the LEGO committee 0x12a43b049A7D330cB8aEAB5113032D18AE9a9030 (9708), the Foundations' multisigs, and the LOL and RCC multisigs.
  - Funds are "only requested based on actual expenses. As a result, any underspent budget has never left the treasury" (9708, post #11), and "The foundation draws funds monthly based on actuals, with a $18M quarterly limit" (9708, post #4).
  - TRP is LDO from the 2020 22M LDO TRP grant, vested through TRP escrow and vesting contracts, not paid in cash from the Agent. In 2025 TRP was transferred to Lido Labs Foundation.

## 3. Spending flows found that the benchmark figures do not include

- Liquidity incentives: Liquidity Rewards / Liquidity Observation Lab (2024: $11.2M, 2025: $4.8M). Before that, reWARDS committee LDO and stETH incentives in 2023. Excluded by definition.
- Rewards Share Program: 3,000 stETH approved in 2023. In 2025, "770 stETH" was distributed to participants (10779). It is partly in Cost of Revenue (deposit referrals 2024 $3.1M, 2025 $4.1M).
- Node operator share of staking rewards and NO rebates: Cost of Revenue, 2024 $51.9M, 2025 $43.1M.
- 2023 TRP LDO vesting, 2023 LEGO grants (quarterly LEGO reports exist separately) and 2023 LOL spending: none appear in any 2023 actuals table found.
- Gas Supply Committee, gas refunds, the bug bounty emergency reserve ($2M, "largely unused") and the Dedicated Bug Bounty Reserve multisig funding in Dec 2025.
- Lido on Solana / Lido-on-X incentives (LIDO-1 era) and the wind-down of the stSOL treasury multisig.
- Delegate Incentivization Programme ($0.3M approved 2025) and Community Lifeguards Initiative ($0.8M approved 2025). These may sit inside Operating Expenses, but they are not shown as separate lines.
- Treasury management actions (stablecoin swaps / Stonks, sUSDS conversion TMC-6) and the planned NEST / liquid buybacks (2025-26 discussions). These are not expenses.
- LDO grants in EGGs (200k LDO in st2024 v1, 180k LDO in st2024 v2) are counted only to the extent they are expensed as TRP or LEGO.
