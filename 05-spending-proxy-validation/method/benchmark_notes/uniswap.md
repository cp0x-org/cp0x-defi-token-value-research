# Uniswap (Uniswap Foundation + Uniswap DAO): reported spending benchmark, 2023-2025

Collected 2026-10-07 from published reports and forum posts only. No on-chain analysis and no DefiLlama data were used.
All UF figures come from the UF's **unaudited** summary financials, posted by UF Head of Finance (forum user `nataliara`).
UF reports present grants as "committed" (new commitments) and "disbursed" (cash paid against commitments). Operating expenses are "accrued" and **exclude employee token awards paid in UNI**, which are reported only in UNI units with no USD value.

## 2. Scope notes

- **UF totals per year (grants disbursed + opex, USD, excluding UNI token awards):** 2023 = $6.72M ($7.08M with legacy UGP $0.36M); 2024 = $15.69M; 2025 = $20.7M. These are sums of the two reported actual components. The UF never states a combined "total spending" figure.
- Employee token awards in UNI (2023: 0.15M UNI; 2024: 0.28M UNI; 2025: 0.45M UNI) are reported in UNI units only, with no USD value, so they are left out of the USD totals.
- All UF reports are **unaudited**. Opex is on an accrual basis ("accrued"). Grants "disbursed" are cash payments against commitments.
- UF grant figures do not cleanly separate out liquidity incentives. The 2023 category "Incentive Programs" (13.8% of disbursements; the 2023 earmark is labelled "Grants commitments and liquidity mining") and 2025 items such as "Morpho: Lending Incentives" are inside grants. No USD split is given, so nothing was removed.
- The FY2024 post states that "some numbers have changed": quarterly commitments add up to $12.66M through Q3, and FY commitments were restated to $14.8M.
- 2025 quarterly opex sums to $9.4M against a reported FY of $9.7M. FY2025 is used as stated.
- The FY2025 figures are pre-UNIfication. The UF's follow-up reply says the $26.3M forward opex projection is not representative going forward.
- Revenue (interest/dividends: 2024 $1.11M, 2025 $1.67M) is not netted against spending.
- Addresses (from published forum posts):

## 3. Other spending flows not included in the UF totals

- **Liquidity incentives (excluded):**
  - Unichain/v4 Liquidity Incentives, $45M managed by Gauntlet (Tally proposal 82, March 2025): https://gov.uniswap.org/t/uniswap-unleashed-unichain-and-uniswap-v4-liquidity-incentives/25250
  - UAC-run Uniswap Revitalization and Growth Program chain incentives: about $4.75M approved in Season 2 plus $142.5k in Merkl fees, and "A total of ~$6 million was distributed via the UAC as of January 20, 2025".
  - Arbitrum LTIPP matching ($750k vote).
  - Uniswap Ecosystem Incentives Initiative (UEII).
  - Unichain Growth Reserve (sequencer revenue the UF directs to growth programs; reported inside UF quarterlies).
- **Buybacks/burns (excluded):** UNIfication (approved 2025-12-26) includes a retroactive burn of 100M UNI from the treasury and fee-switch burns.
- **Budgets starting after the period:** UNIfication growth budget of 20M UNI per year to Uniswap Labs, vested quarterly from 2026-01-01 (outside 2023-2025).
- **Transfers to UF (not spending):** $43.41M (2023) and 20.3M UNI under Unleashed (booked as $140M in Q1 2025). Spending out of these transfers already appears in the UF grants and opex rows.
- **DUNI $16.5M legal/tax prefund:** a budget allocation to a DAO entity. No published report of actual 2025 DUNI spending was found.
- **UF loan:** a $29M loan against 5M UNI collateral (Q1/Q2 2025). This is a financing flow, not spending.
