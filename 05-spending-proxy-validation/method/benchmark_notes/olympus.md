# Olympus DAO (OHM): reported operating spending benchmark, 2023–2025

Collected 2026-10-07. Sources are only forum.olympusdao.finance posts (read through the Flarum JSON API, `/api/discussions/<id>`) and the matching Snapshot proposal texts (space `olympusdao.eth`). I did no on-chain analysis and used no DefiLlama data.

Key finding: Olympus doesn't publish calendar-year financial statements. Since 2023 the Olympus Association (a non-profit) has received budgets through OIP/OOP votes, and it reports actual spend only through a Google Looker Studio dashboard ("Olympus DAO Allocation Insights"). The dashboard needs JavaScript and I couldn't read it from this environment (the WebFetch permission request timed out). Fiscal years don't line up with calendar years. FY2023 runs Jan 2023 to Jan 2024, FY2024 runs Feb 2024 to Jan 2025, and FY2025 runs Feb 2025 to Feb 2026. No quarterly reports for 2023–2025 were found on the forum. OIP-128 mentions a Q4/2022 report, but nothing after it turned up.

Calendar-year answer:
- **2023**: actual spend isn't stated directly. Reported budget is $5,980,000. The reported savings of $817k apply to the 13-month period Jan 2023 to Jan 2024 (rows 1 and 4). There are no H1/H2 actuals. The H1 and H2 amounts (2.07M and 3.91M) are funding transfers, not spend.
- **2024**: no actual figure found in text. Budget for Feb 2024 to Jan 2025 is $888k expected plus $70.5k marketing ($1,429k plus $75.35k with contingency). Feb 2024 isn't a calendar year, and Jan 2024 falls under the 2023 framework.
- **2025**: FY Feb 2025 to Feb 2026 actual is $1.33M (contributors $1.16M), quoted from the dashboard by a community member and implicitly confirmed by the Association. No calendar-2025 split was found. The only sub-period figure is "Q4 alone was ~$438K", which I couldn't map to calendar quarters.
- I didn't sum any quarters or halves, because none of the actual sub-period sets are complete.

## (2) Scope notes
- **Paying entity**: from Q4 2022, the DAO funds the **Olympus Association** (a non-profit, set up under OIP-124) in OHM ("DAI in OHM" / "USD in OHM equivalent"). The Association pays contributors, legal, subscriptions and audits. OHM is minted or transferred from the DAO/treasury to the Association. Per OOP-2 post #7, "OHM is only added to the circulating supply when allocations go out." OIP-128 references the DAO holding wallet https://etherscan.io/address/0x245cc372c84b3645bf0ffe6538620b04a217988b ("The DAO currently holds 1,559,510 OHM … visible here"). I didn't analyse it. Grants funds (~$500k gOHM from OIPs 55/90/108) were moved to the Association under OIP-135 (2023).
- From FY2025 there is no up-front funding transfer. The Association gets "incremental" funding plus a 500,000 OHM credit line (OIP-179). Credit-line OTC sales of OHM (99,000 OHM net in FY2025) are financing, not expense.
- Budgets exclude variable items (audits under OIP-98 with up to $500k/yr, Immunefi bug bounties, unforeseen legal, 3rd-party services). It isn't clear from any text whether the actual figures include them.
- Fiscal years don't match calendar years (Jan 2023–Jan 2024, Feb 2024–Jan 2025, Feb 2025–Feb 2026).
- The FY2025 actual includes a one-off reimbursement covering 2022 to date ("There was a 2022 to date service reimbursements payment in Q4 (google, figma, notion, etc)", 5226 post #3) and Callisto spend that's described as a loan.
- Headcount, per 5226 post #3: "Olympus has 7 full time contributors and 1 part time contributor"; "Engineering 37%, Marketing 24%, Ops/Admin/BD 39%".

## (3) Other spending flows not included
- Hypernative security monitoring, a separate OIP and not part of the Association budget figures above: $81.6k/12 months (OIP-134, Mar 2023), $60k/12 months (OIP-156, Mar 2024), $60k/12 months (OIP-180, Mar 2025), all paid in OHM. These are budgets only.
- Grants program: over its lifetime "Total of 629,447 USD of OHM awarded" (OIP-135, Mar 2023, mostly 2022). New 2023 grants were funded from existing ~$500k gOHM, with no new allocation.
- Liquidity incentives: OIP-178 FY25 Liquidity and Incentivization Budget, "not to exceed amount of 2,000,000 OHM" (Berachain incentives). Excluded as liquidity incentives.
- Excluded by definition: RBS market operations, Yield Repurchase Facility buybacks (OIP-164/165/174), Cooler Loans flows, POL deployments, the consolidation-users make-whole payment (OIP-167), staking.
- Association OTC/credit line (OIP-179/193): financing flows.
- BitGo custody fees for Berachain holdings: mentioned as unbudgeted spend, with no amount given.
